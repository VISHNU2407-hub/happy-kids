About Our School

A nurturing campus where every learner is seen, heard & supported

Happy Kids High School is a co-educational, private unaided (recognized) Upper Primary and Secondary institution located in Shakarajupalle Village, Sidhout Block, Y.S.R. Kadapa District, Andhra Pradesh. We serve students from Nursery to Class 10 with a structured curriculum and values-based learning.

As a rural campus serving the local community, we are recognized by the relevant education authorities and continue to grow — currently operational and committed to quality education for every child who walks through our gates.
